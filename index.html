GOOD TO KNOW

Before you arrive.

When can I check in and out?

Check-in is from 2 pm. Check-out is by 12 noon. Reception is available 24 hours; contact us about early arrival or late departure.

Can we book interconnected rooms?

Yes, some rooms have an internal connecting door. We also have adjoining rooms. Ask reception to confirm the arrangement and total price for your dates.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

Reception will confirm deposit, payment and cancellation terms with your quote before you commit to a booking.
