YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy & analytics

This website provides information about Embassy Lodge at House 22, Street 4, F-6/3, Islamabad. Contact reception on +92 302 8508850 with privacy questions.

Booking enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message containing your selected dates and room requirements. It does not submit a booking or take payment. These details are passed to WhatsApp when you choose to enquire; you decide whether to send the message. Your enquiry is then handled through WhatsApp and reception.

Optional Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, this site loads Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-K84HNTXJLX). It measures website visits and interactions such as WhatsApp, call, directions, gallery and external-link clicks. Google may process device, browser and visit information and store analytics cookies. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in this site's configuration.

We do not send the enquiry form's dates, guest counts, message text, names or phone numbers as custom analytics events. A button click indicates an action on the website, not a confirmed booking. Standard campaign parameters may be used to understand where visitors came from.

Analytics only loads after you choose “Allow analytics”. Your choice is saved in this browser's local storage. You can change it through “Analytics preferences” at the bottom of the home page. Declining prevents future analytics events from this site; it does not erase previously collected data. You can clear existing cookies in your browser.

Other services

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, Booking.com, Instagram and the city activities guide open services with their own privacy practices. The website's hosting provider may also process basic request information to serve the pages. No account or payment details are requested on this website.
